# Online Help Center

TV Manager v18.3.0 adds a first version of built-in online help.

## Pages

- `/help` — help index, workflow map, and SickChill parity matrix.
- `/help/getting-started` — first-run and cutover workflow.
- `/help/show-queue` — missing/downloaded queue behavior.
- `/help/episode-management` — bulk episode management.
- `/help/ignore-rules` — ignored episodes, S00 specials, and queue/search math.
- `/help/download-center` — downloader testing and queue handoff.
- `/help/post-processing` — completed download workflow.
- `/help/subtitles` — subtitle scan guidance.
- `/help/troubleshooting` — Jobs, Logs, Database Safety, and System diagnostics.

## Product rule

Every major screen should explain:

1. what it does,
2. why it matters,
3. what to do next,
4. where progress appears,
5. where errors are logged.

v18.3.0 starts this with a centralized Help Center and workflow map. Later releases should add smaller field-level help and walkthrough callouts directly inside each complex form.
